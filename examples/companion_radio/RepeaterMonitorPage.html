Clock offset uses existing login replies or clock replies received during manual sync. Wall-clock replies have minute precision and show an approximate offset with uncertainty. Clock measurements use existing replies. Unavailable means no clock measurement has been recorded. Positive means ahead; warnings exceed 30 seconds plus round-trip uncertainty. Repeater timestamp counters can add bias. Sync time reuses confirmed admin access. If a repeater refuses a backward change and its wall clock is more than a minute ahead, it may be reset/rebooted once, then synced again. Sunrise checks also sync time when no successful sync is recorded, the last sync is at least 30 days old, or the latest measured offset is ±10 minutes or more. Sync requires a saved password and recent NTP time; only one automatic attempt is made per repeater each day. Administrators can also use Sync time manually. History keeps today and the preceding six local dates. A new check replaces that date’s earlier result. Failed checks are never recorded as zero volts. If the bot starts after sunrise, it checks remaining repeaters once time is synchronized.
